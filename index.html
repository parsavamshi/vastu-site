About

Lord Vishwakarma

Vishwakarma is a revered deity in Hinduism known as the divine architect and master craftsman of the universe. He is believed to be the creator of celestial cities, palaces, and powerful weapons used by the gods.

In Hindu mythology, Vishwakarma is associated with engineering, design, construction, and creativity. He is said to have built famous mythological places like the golden city of Lanka and the sacred city of Dwarka, and crafted divine weapons such as Indra’s Vajra and Vishnu’s Sudarshan Chakra.

He is widely worshipped by engineers, architects, artisans, mechanics, and craftsmen, who see him as their guiding deity. During Vishwakarma Puja, people pray to him for success, safety, and excellence in their work.

In simple terms, Vishwakarma represents the spirit of creation, skill, and innovation, combining both art and science in building and designing the world.
