Lord Vishwakarma –

Vishwakarma is a revered deity in Hindu tradition, honored as the divine architect and master craftsman of the universe. He symbolizes the highest form of creativity, engineering, and design—where art and science come together to shape the world.

According to ancient texts and mythology, Vishwakarma is believed to be the creator of magnificent celestial cities, palaces, and powerful weapons used by the gods. His extraordinary skill and vision are reflected in legendary creations such as the golden city of Lanka and the sacred city of Dwarka, along with divine weapons like Indra’s Vajra and Vishnu’s Sudarshan Chakra.

Beyond mythology, Vishwakarma represents the principles of construction, innovation, and precision. He is deeply associated with fields such as architecture, engineering, craftsmanship and mechanical arts.

Even today, Vishwakarma is widely worshipped by engineers, architects, artisans, mechanics and creators of all kinds, who look to him as a guiding force for excellence in their work. During the festival of Vishwakarma Puja, tools, machines and workplaces are honored, symbolizing respect for skill, effort and creation.
